PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated August 29, 2026



This Privacy Notice for Sems Inc ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:
  • Download and use our mobile application (Run Dino), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice.
  • Use Run Dino. The Run Dino game features dynamic gameplay where the score increases by 40 to 50 points per second, and successfully clearing obstacles awards an additional 300 points. The game speed gradually increases as the player progresses. A background transition to a night theme occurs at a score of 8,000, and switches back to a day theme at 14,000. Settings include three distinct control modes (Control A, Control B, and Control C) to suit player preferences, along with options to toggle sound effects and vibration feedback.
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or events.
Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at semsinc.support@gmail.com.
